Signals Beneath the Salt

By Arun Nwosu

The listening station heard the first pulse at 03:17, beneath a kilometer of wind-polished salt.

It repeated every eighty-one seconds: three short tones, a pause, and one sustained note.

The second instrument

When the geophone was moved north, the pulse arrived two seconds earlier. The source was not in the sky.

The team logged the anomaly and waited for daylight.
